# Resources for students

- The mixer is available at [composingatmospheres.ro/mixer](https://composingatmospheres.ro/mixer) and you can play around to create your sounds.
    - To play a cell, press the **play** button. For each cell you have **volume** and **panning** (left, right) controls
- [Here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1GniWG-FP7ZKbSEtPcKGtz0DEHewvg_cK?usp=sharing) is a drive with `.wav` files of the sounds that are in the mixer.

- You can try [this online DAW](https://opendaw.org/) or [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/), simple DAWs to try out your ideas.

- A [bit about](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambient_music) ambient music, relevant to our musical experiments. Personal recommandation - you can listen to:
    - **Brain Eno**
    - **Steve Roach**
    - **Stars of the Lid**

- The album cover for [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sgjwiadze1w) album is a building. Architecture is an inspiration for musicians.
