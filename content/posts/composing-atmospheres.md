# Resources for students

- The mixer is available at [composingatmospheres.ro/mixer](https://composingatmospheres.ro/mixer) and you can play around to create your sounds.
    - To play a cell, press the **play** button. For each cell you have **volume** and **panning** (left, right) controls
- [Here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1GniWG-FP7ZKbSEtPcKGtz0DEHewvg_cK?usp=sharing) is a drive with `.wav` files of the sounds that are in the mixer.

# Composing

## Ideas
Composing architecture-inspired music is certainly an interesting process. My take on it is that we can understand the space at different depth levels ranging from **concrete** (placement sfx, material qualities, etc.) to **abstract** (understanding the space as a whole, like telling a story about the space).

Concrete way of thinking about space should give us a more generally available result, while abstract tends to be more about the vision of the composer.
